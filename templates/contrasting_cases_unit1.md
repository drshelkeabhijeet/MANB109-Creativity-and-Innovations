# Contrasting Cases — Unit 1
## Problem statements and idea lists: which is stronger, and why?

Used in **Session 2** (problem sentences) and **Session 4** (idea lists). Read both examples in
each pair. Decide which is stronger. **Write down why** before anyone tells you. The point is
not to guess the answer — it is to build your own sense of what good looks like.

> दोन्ही वाचा. कोणते चांगले आहे ते ठरवा. *का* ते लिहा.

## Pair 2 — Two brainstorm lists for redesigning a tailor's digital front door

**A.** (5 ideas, 10 minutes)
1. Make a website
2. Instagram page
3. Google listing
4. WhatsApp number on the board outside
5. Online booking

**B.** (20 ideas, 10 minutes)
1. Website · 2. Instagram · 3. Google listing · 4. WhatsApp on the board · 5. Online booking ·
6. Before/after photos of alterations on WhatsApp status · 7. A voice note explaining prices
in Marathi · 8. QR code on every receipt linking to the Maps pin · 9. **Stop** taking phone
orders, WhatsApp only · 10. A photo of the *measurement book* so customers trust the record ·
11. Send a "your blouse is ready" WhatsApp instead of waiting for them to call · 12. List
turnaround time publicly · 13. Show the sewing machine, not the shopfront · 14. Ask every
customer for a Google review while they pay · 15. Group WhatsApp for the wedding-season
customers · 16. Post the day's *finished* pieces at 7 pm · 17. Delete the Instagram nobody
updates · 18. A pinned WhatsApp catalogue of the ten standard jobs and prices · 19. Put the
owner's face on the listing, not a stock image · 20. Offer to photograph the customer wearing
the finished piece

| | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| How many ideas are *removals* rather than additions? | | |
| How many could the owner do this week for free? | | |
| How many would you have thought of yourself? | | |
| Where do the surprising ideas sit — early or late in the list? | | |

**Which list would you rather choose from, and why?**
