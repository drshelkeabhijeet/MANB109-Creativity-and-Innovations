# Peer Critique Protocol
## MANB109 Creativity and Innovations

Runs in every session after the pitches. Ten minutes. Not graded — this is feedback, not
assessment.

---

## The rule

**Critique the idea. Never the person.** Say "this idea assumes X" — never "you didn't think
about X".

## The three moves

Every responder gives one of each, in this order. No general praise, no "it's good".

| Move | Marathi | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **I like…** | मला हे आवडलं… | Name one specific thing that works, and *why* it works |
| **I wish…** | मला असं वाटतं की… | Name one specific thing that would make it stronger |
| **What if…** | जर असं केलं तर? | Offer one new possibility — not a fix, a fork |

> Marathi phrasings are a starting point — adjust to how your students actually speak.

## Timing

| | |
|---|---|
| Pitch | 3 min |
| Two responders, three moves each | 4 min |
| Presenter responds — one sentence only, no defending | 1 min |
| Next | |

## The presenter's job

Write down the "I wish" and the "What if" in your idea log. **Do not argue.** You are not
defending a position; you are collecting material. You may decide later that the critique was
wrong — record it anyway.

## The facilitator's job

- Enforce "idea, not person" the first time it slips. It only takes once.
- In weeks 1–2, take critique on **team** output only. No individual is singled out before
  trust exists.
- If the room goes quiet, model one full set of three moves yourself, then hand it back.
