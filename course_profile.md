### Mapping Justifications

- **109.1 → PO1(2), PO3(1), PO6(2):** Grasping the creative process and the creativity-to-innovation link equips students to frame problems before deciding (PO1). The Unit 1 activity redesigns a mobile app or website, giving light exposure to digital product thinking (PO3). Creativity is taught as the foundation of enterprise renewal, directly relevant to MSMEs and local businesses (PO6).

- **109.2 → PO3(2), PO5(3), PO6(2):** Presenting ideas through storytelling and visual tools is squarely a communication competence, exercised in every studio pitch (PO5). Visual articulation uses digital mind-mapping and presentation tools (PO3), and the ideas being pitched are enterprise and startup propositions (PO6).

- **109.3 → PO1(3), PO2(2), PO3(2), PO5(1), PO6(3):** SCAMPER is a structured generation-and-selection method: students produce alternatives and choose among them, which is managerial decision-making (PO1). Its seven prompts force systematic examination of a product across dimensions (PO2), applied in Unit 3 to a tech product (PO3). Team outputs are defended aloud (PO5), and the object throughout is improving a real product or service (PO6).

- **109.4 → PO1(2), PO2(3), PO3(2), PO5(2), PO6(3):** Mind mapping is taught explicitly to break complex problems into manageable parts, which is structured problem diagnosis (PO2), and the resulting decomposition informs what to act on first (PO1). Unit 2 applies it to technology startup ideas (PO3). Collaborative mind mapping is a shared visual artefact teams reason through together (PO5), and the worked example is mapping the components needed to launch a venture (PO6).

> **PO4 is mapped 0 by all four COs.** Nothing in the prescribed syllabus addresses ethical,
> social, or inclusive leadership, and inflating a mapping to fill the column would misstate

> **On CO 109.2 and the three-unit syllabus.** No unit names storytelling or visual

> visual tool. This reading is defensible, but it should be recorded with the Board of Studies

## Syllabus

### Unit 1: Foundations of Creativity and Innovation — 10 hours

**Key Topics:**
- **Introduction to Creativity:** Definition, importance, and role in problem-solving.
- **Creativity vs. Innovation:** How creative thinking leads to innovation in business.
- **The Creative Process:** Stages of creativity — from idea generation to implementation.
- **Barriers to Creativity:** How to overcome mental blocks and traditional thinking.

**Hands-on Activity:** Brainstorm ideas to redesign a basic mobile app or website and create a creative solution.

---

### Unit 2: Mind Mapping and Creative Thinking Techniques — 10 hours

**Key Topics:**
- **Introduction to Mind Mapping:** Basics of mind mapping, its benefits, and how it helps with idea generation.
- **Mind Mapping for Problem Solving:** Using mind maps to break down complex tech issues into manageable parts.
- **Collaborative Mind Mapping:** How teams can use mind mapping as a creative and collaborative tool for projects.

**Hands-on Activity:** Create a mind map for a technology startup idea, visually breaking down the components needed to launch it.

---

### Unit 3: SCAMPER Technique for Innovation — 10 hours

**Key Topics:**
- **Introduction to SCAMPER:** Understanding SCAMPER (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Rearrange) as a creativity tool.
- **Using SCAMPER for Product Improvement:** Applying SCAMPER to improve existing products or services.
- **Team SCAMPER Exercise:** Working in groups to apply SCAMPER to a real-world business or tech problem.

**Hands-on Activity:** Teams apply SCAMPER to redesign or enhance a tech product (e.g., a mobile app or website).
