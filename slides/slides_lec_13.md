## 2. The one idea  `CONCEPT`

Run the prompts in silence first, then talk. Reverse that order and the loudest person's grid becomes the team's grid.

> Speaker notes: Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

---

## 3. Why it matters  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Silent first, always** — Each member fills the whole grid alone before anyone speaks.
- **Then merge, prompt by prompt** — Compare Substitute rows before anyone touches Combine.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

## 4. What to do with it  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Disagreement means the row is alive** — Two different answers on one prompt is better than four identical ones.
- **Pick the problem carefully** — Real, specific, and one nobody at the table has already solved.
