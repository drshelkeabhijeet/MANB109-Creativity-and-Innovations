3Why it matters

What changed — Not the business — you. Fifteen weeks ago you asked different questions.

The hand-back is short — One page, in the owner's language, saying what you saw and what you would do first.

Notes Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

4What to do with it

No jargon — Not one word from this course appears in the hand-back. No SCAMPER, no BOI, no mind map.

One recommendation — Not five. The one you would stand behind if they did it and it failed.
