2The one idea

The good idea is not the hard part. The hard part is everything that happens after it.

Notes Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

3Why it matters

The stages — Preparation, incubation, illumination, verification, implementation. Illumination is the one everybody romanticises and the one that takes the least time.

Incubation is real work — The idea that arrives in the bus is paid for by the two hours you spent stuck the day before.

Notes Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

4What to do with it

Where ideas actually die — Two gaps: between illumination and verification (no cheap way to test), and between verification and implementation (it works, nobody does it).

Locating the stall — Your anchor's owner is stuck at exactly one of these stages. Naming which one is today's work.

Notes Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

5Today you will

Find out which stage your anchor's owner is stuck at, and write the problem in one sentence you could act on.

Marathi or Hindi · 60 minutes

Notes Leave this slide up for the whole making phase.

6To close

Pitch — Three minutes. The person, the stuck, the idea, the picture, the ask.

Critique — I like… / I wish… / What if… — the idea, never the person.

Idea log — One page. Silent. Box 4 is the one you read again in week 15.

Notes If time runs short, cut the input — never the pitch. The pitch is how CO 109.2 is attained.
