Notes Studio session. Warm-up: The Worst Website. Do not lecture past 15 minutes.

2The one idea

Your customer's first contact with this business is already digital, whether the owner designed it or not.

Notes Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

3Why it matters

The front door is rarely a website — For most businesses here it is a WhatsApp number, a Google Maps pin, or a photo someone else uploaded.

It exists already, unmanaged — Search your anchor right now — whatever comes up is the front door, including nothing at all.

Notes Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

4What to do with it

Redesign means the whole first contact, not a ho — mepage: what a customer sees, how they ask, how they pay, how they come back.

Cheap beats impressive — A correct Google listing with photos and real hours outperforms a website nobody visits.

Notes Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

5Today you will

Brainstorm ideas to redesign a basic mobile app or website and create a creative solution — for your anchor's own digital front door.
