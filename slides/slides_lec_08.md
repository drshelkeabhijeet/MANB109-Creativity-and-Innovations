## 2. The one idea  `CONCEPT`

The person who knows least about your business will draw the branch you cannot see.

> Speaker notes: Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

## 3. Why it matters  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Buzan's group sequence** — Individual maps first, silently. Only then a shared map. Reversing the order lets one voice set the BOIs for everyone.
- **Outsiders are the asset** — Your teammates have never met your owner. Their ignorance is why they will ask the branch you stopped asking.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

---

## 4. What to do with it  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Disagreement is data** — Two people putting the same idea on different branches means the structure is not settled — that is worth more than agreement.
- **One scribe, rotating** — Everyone talks, one draws, and the drawer changes each round.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.
