## 2. The one idea  `CONCEPT`

Every business runs on assumptions nobody has questioned in years. Your job is to find one and turn it upside down.

> Speaker notes: Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

---

## 3. Why it matters  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Assumptions are invisible from inside** — The owner cannot see them because they are the water. You can, because you arrived last week.
- **Reversal, not criticism** — 'Customers want low prices' becomes 'what if they wanted high prices?' — you are not saying the owner is wrong, you are testing whether the rule is load-bearing.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

---

## 4. What to do with it  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Most reversals fail, and that is fine** — Nine will be nonsense. The tenth reorganises the business.
- **The one that scares you is the signal** — If a reversal makes you uncomfortable, it is touching something real.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.
