## 2. The one idea  `CONCEPT`

A startup is not an idea. It is a list of things that must all exist at the same time.

> Speaker notes: Say it once. Do not elaborate on the slide — elaborate out loud.

---

## 3. Why it matters  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Launch components as BOIs** — Offering, customer, channel, money, people, legal, technology. Four to seven of these become the main branches.
- **The map exposes what you have not thought about** — A thin branch is not a small problem; it is an unexamined one.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.

---

## 4. What to do with it  `FRAMEWORK`

- **Tie it to your anchor** — The startup is either the digital version of what your owner already does, or the adjacent thing they could start.
- **Buzan's laws still apply** — Central image, one word per line, curved branches, landscape.

> Speaker notes: Four beats across two slides. Fifteen minutes for all of it.
