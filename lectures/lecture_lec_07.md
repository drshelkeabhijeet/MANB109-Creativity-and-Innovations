# Session 07 — Mapping to Break a Problem Down

**Unit:** 2 · Mind Mapping and Creative Thinking Techniques
**Duration:** 120 min · **COs:** 109.4, 109.2
**Session type:** Studio (practical)

> The map turns from describing a business to dissecting one problem. The output is an action small enough to do this week.

## 0:00 – 0:10 · Recall, then warm-up — "Map Something Complicated"

### 0:00 – 0:03 · Recall — from memory, log closed

Log closed, from memory, said aloud to a partner:

> Say three of Buzan's laws without looking. Which one did your first map break, and what did the second map fix?

Three minutes. Do not correct anyone; the effort of retrieving is the point, not accuracy. Retrieval from memory is what moves last session's learning from short-term to long-term — rereading the log does not.

### 0:03 – 0:10 · Warm-up

**What you do.**

> "Five minutes, Buzan's laws, landscape. Map a wedding. Or a bus route. Or admission day at this college."

One law reminder before they start: central image, one word per line, curved branches.

**Why it is here.** Practises the laws on something with no emotional weight before turning them on the anchor's real problem.

---

## 0:10 – 0:25 · Input

**The one idea students must leave with:**

> **You cannot act on 'sales are down'. You can act on 'nobody within two streets knows we deliver'.**

**Say roughly this — four beats, not a lecture:**

1. **The centre changes.** Last session the centre was the business. Today it is the problem — the sentence you wrote in Session 2.
2. **BOIs become causes.** Four to seven candidate reasons this problem exists. Not solutions yet.
3. **Keep splitting until it is small.** A leaf you can act on this week without permission or money.
4. **Most problems are three small problems** wearing one big name.

**Do not** run past fifteen minutes. If it will not fit, move it into the making phase as something students discover rather than something you tell them.

---

## 0:25 – 1:25 · Making

**The task, as you will state it:**

> "Map one anchor problem until you reach a leaf you could act on this week."

**Language:** Marathi or Hindi for divergent work. Only the pitch is in English.

### 0:25 – 0:50 · Map the problem (25 min)

A3 landscape, Buzan's laws. Central image of the *problem*, not the business. BOIs are candidate causes. Split each until the branches get specific.

### 0:50 – 1:05 · Find the actionable leaf (15 min)

Circle every leaf you could act on within a week with no money. Most maps have two or three. If yours has none, you have not split far enough.

### 1:05 – 1:25 · Swap and find another (20 min)

Trade maps with a partner. Find a leaf they circled — and one they did not. Explain why you would start there.

### Circulating — what good looks like

- A leaf phrased as a verb
- Someone splitting one branch four levels deep while others stop at two
- A partner finding an obvious leaf the owner of the map missed

### What stuck looks like, and the nudge

| If you see | Say |
|---|---|
| Branches are solutions, not causes | "That is an answer. What is the question it answers? Put that on the branch." |
| Map stops at two levels | "Take the vaguest branch and ask 'why' three more times." |
| No actionable leaf | "Everything here needs money. Split until you find something that needs only an afternoon." |
| Central image is the business again | "The centre is the problem today. Redraw the middle." |

**Do not solve it for them.** Ask a question and walk away.

### If someone finishes early

In creative work, finishing early usually means stopping too soon. Hand them this:

> Map the same problem from the customer's side. Are the causes the same ones?

## 1:25 – 1:50 · Critique and pitch

3 minutes. The pitch is the actionable leaf and why you would start there — beat 3 is one action, not a strategy.

Run `templates/peer_critique_protocol.md` — I like / I wish / What if.

> This closing beat is the attainment path for **CO 109.2**. If time runs short, cut the input, never the pitch.

> Write the leaf you chose. In Session 15 you will check whether it was the right one.
