# Session 08 — Collaborative Mind Mapping

**Unit:** 2 · Mind Mapping and Creative Thinking Techniques
**Duration:** 120 min · **COs:** 109.4, 109.2
**Session type:** Studio (practical)

> Buzan's group process: individual maps first, then a shared one. The value is the branch an outsider draws that the owner of the business could not see.

## 0:00 – 0:10 · Warm-up — "The Silent Map"

**What you do.**

Teams of four, one A3 sheet, one marker each, **no talking at all**. Five minutes. Topic: this college.

Then thirty seconds of talking about what was hard.

**Why it is here.** Removes the loudest voice from the process. Teams discover they can build a shared structure without anyone directing it — which is exactly Buzan's argument for mapping before discussing.

---

## 0:10 – 0:25 · Input

**The one idea students must leave with:**

> **The person who knows least about your business will draw the branch you cannot see.**

**Say roughly this — four beats, not a lecture:**

1. **Buzan's group sequence.** Individual maps first, silently. Only then a shared map. Reversing the order lets one voice set the BOIs for everyone.
2. **Outsiders are the asset.** Your teammates have never met your owner. Their ignorance is why they will ask the branch you stopped asking.
3. **Disagreement is data.** Two people putting the same idea on different branches means the structure is not settled — that is worth more than agreement.
4. **One scribe, rotating.** Everyone talks, one draws, and the drawer changes each round.

**Do not** run past fifteen minutes. If it will not fit, move it into the making phase as something students discover rather than something you tell them.

---

## 0:25 – 1:25 · Making

**The task, as you will state it:**

> "Your team maps each member's anchor in turn. You are the outsider three times and the owner once."

**Language:** Marathi or Hindi for divergent work. Only the pitch is in English.

### 0:25 – 0:55 · Four rounds, seven minutes each (30 min)

Teams of four. One member's anchor per round: the owner gives sixty seconds of context, then stays silent while the other three map it. Rotate.

### 0:55 – 1:10 · What surprised you (15 min)

Each owner reads their team's map of their own business. Write down the one branch you would not have drawn.

### 1:10 – 1:25 · Fold it in (15 min)

Add that branch to your own map from Session 6 or 7, in a different colour so you can see later that it came from outside.

### Circulating — what good looks like

- A team map with a branch the owner visibly did not expect
- The owner staying silent for the full seven minutes
- Someone adding an outsider's branch even though they disagree with it

### What stuck looks like, and the nudge

| If you see | Say |
|---|---|
| Owner keeps correcting the team | "You are silent this round. Write your objections down — you can use them after." |
| Team maps only what the owner said | "You have never been to this shop. What would you want to know as a customer?" |
| One person holding the marker all four rounds | "Rotate. Hand it left." |
| Team finishes in three minutes | "You have four more. Take the thinnest branch and grow it." |

**Do not solve it for them.** Ask a question and walk away.

---

## 1:25 – 1:50 · Critique and pitch

3 minutes. Pitch the branch you did not draw yourself — what your team saw that you missed, and what you will do about it.

Run `templates/peer_critique_protocol.md` — I like / I wish / What if.

> This closing beat is the attainment path for **CO 109.2**. If time runs short, cut the input, never the pitch.
