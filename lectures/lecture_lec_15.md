# Session 15 — Final Pitch and the Look-Back

**Unit:** 3 · SCAMPER Technique for Innovation
**Duration:** 120 min · **COs:** 109.2, 109.3, 109.4
**Session type:** Studio (practical)

> Closes the semester. Two jobs: the final pitch, and the week-15 look-back that shows students how far their own thinking moved. The hand-back to the owner is what makes fifteen weeks of work land on a real person.

---

- [ ] Printed, one per student: `templates/pitch_structure.md`

## 0:00 – 0:10 · Warm-up — "Read Yourself From Week One"

**What you do.**

> "Open your idea log at Session 1, Box 4. Read what you wrote about this business to your partner. Out loud."

Then: "Would you write that today?"

**Why it is here.** The single most effective moment in the course. Students hear their own week-one thinking and measure the distance without anyone grading them.

---

## 0:10 – 0:25 · Input

**The one idea students must leave with:**

> **You owe the owner an answer, not a report.**

**Say roughly this — four beats, not a lecture:**

1. **What changed.** Not the business — you. Fifteen weeks ago you asked different questions.
2. **The hand-back is short.** One page, in the owner's language, saying what you saw and what you would do first.
3. **No jargon.** Not one word from this course appears in the hand-back. No SCAMPER, no BOI, no mind map.
4. **One recommendation.** Not five. The one you would stand behind if they did it and it failed.

**Do not** run past fifteen minutes. If it will not fit, move it into the making phase as something students discover rather than something you tell them.

---

## 0:25 – 1:25 · Making

**The task, as you will state it:**

> "Assemble your portfolio and write the one-page hand-back you will give your owner."

**Language:** Marathi or Hindi for divergent work. Only the pitch is in English.

### 0:25 – 0:50 · Assemble the portfolio (25 min)

Every artefact in order: the anchor brief, the maps, the SCAMPER grids, the sketches. Add one line to each saying what it told you.

### 0:50 – 1:10 · Write the hand-back (20 min)

One page, in the owner's language. What you saw, one recommendation, what it costs, what to do first. No course vocabulary.

### 1:10 – 1:25 · Rehearse in pairs (15 min)

Read the hand-back aloud to a partner as if they were the owner. Partner asks one question the owner would ask. Fix that sentence.

### Circulating — what good looks like

- A hand-back with no course jargon in it
- One recommendation, not a list
- A student who realises their week-one problem statement was wrong and says so

### What stuck looks like, and the nudge

| If you see | Say |
|---|---|
| Hand-back is a report | "They will not read three pages. What is the one thing you would say if you had two minutes at their counter?" |
| Full of course terms | "Would your owner know what a BOI is? Rewrite that line as you would say it out loud." |
| Five recommendations | "Which one first? Only that one goes on the page." |
| "Nothing changed since week one" | "Read Box 4 again. You wrote 'they need a website'. Do you still think that?" |

**Do not solve it for them.** Ask a question and walk away.

---

## 1:25 – 1:50 · Critique and pitch

**Final portfolio pitch.** Full structure, 3 minutes. Hold up the artefact that changed your mind. The room's critique is the last of the semester — make it generous and specific.

Run `templates/peer_critique_protocol.md` — I like / I wish / What if.

> This closing beat is the attainment path for **CO 109.2**. If time runs short, cut the input, never the pitch.

---

## 1:50 – 2:00 · Idea log

Students fill the Session 15 page of `templates/idea_log.md`. Silent — do not talk over it.

> Complete the **week-15 look-back** section: what you wrote in Session 1, what you would write now, and whether what changed was the enterprise or you.

## After the course

Deliver the hand-back to your owner. In person if you can. They gave you fifteen weeks of material.

---

## If you are short on time

Cut the rehearsal. Never cut the look-back or the hand-back.

## Room size

- **Under 10 students:** everyone pitches; add a second critique round.
- **Over 40:** six pitch today. Track it and rotate — do not let the confident students take every slot.
